# Code of Conduct

hams follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than people, and
accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution. Harassment, insults, and personal or
political attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project
space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/muneebsajjad). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, as
described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
